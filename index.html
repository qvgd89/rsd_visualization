Velocity components

Host halo motion αh
Satellite motion αs

Zs = Z + (1+z)/H(z) [αh VZ,host + αs (VZ − VZ,host)]
Centrals move with their host (VZ = VZ,host), so only the satellites are affected by αs.
Dark matter particles have no host decomposition: their full velocity is weighted by αh.

Zs = Z + α (1+z)/H(z) VZ
 (1+z)/H(z) = (Mpc/h)/(km/s)
Zs wraps periodically in the Mpc/h box.
